Profile

  • 7+ years building genomics, transcriptomics and systems-modelling pipelines for academic and international research teams in Czechia, the USA and Switzerland.
  • Built Augusta, an open-source Python package that infers gene regulatory and Boolean networks from RNA-Seq data, enabling genome-scale modelling even for non-model organisms.
  • Assembled and annotated the first complete genomes of five thermophilic bacteria: four strains of the genus Caldimonas/Schlegelella and Aneurinibacillus thermoaerophilus CCM 8960. Phylogenomic analysis of these genomes clarified the taxonomy of the group and gave bioplastics research a reference set to build on.
  • Co-described eight novel bacterial species, among them Corynebacterium and Rothia species isolated from Antarctic wildlife.
  • Principal Investigator of three funded projects worth 300,700 EUR, including a Czech Science Foundation postdoctoral grant hosted at ETH Zurich; Co-Investigator or team member on four further projects.
  • Authored 13 peer-reviewed journal articles (5 as first author) and 37 conference contributions, presented at ISMB/ECCB, LS2 and ESBP.
  • Named Forbes Top Czech Woman Scientist 2025 and recognised by the L'Oréal-UNESCO For Women in Science Czechia jury for contributing to the reduction of animal testing through in silico approaches.
